60-day recovery roadmap

Phase 1 · Foundation recoveryDays 1–20

Build concepts, NCERT understanding, solved examples and basic question confidence. Start with manageable, prerequisite-light chapters.

  • Check syllabus and establish baseline.
  • Study selected chapters in all three subjects.
  • Practise basic questions and chapter-wise PYQs.
  • Start formula, reaction/concept and mistake notebooks.
Phase 2 · Question practiceDays 21–40

Turn understanding into accuracy and speed.

  • Increase JEE Main PYQ practice.
  • Revise completed chapters and repair weak concepts.
  • Take chapter-wise and subject-wise tests.
  • Continue CBSE written-answer practice.
Phase 3 · Exam readinessDays 41–60

Practise exam execution, question selection and revision.

  • Attempt timed mock tests.
  • Analyse every mock and maintain an error log.
  • Revise formulas, NCERT and recurring mistakes.
  • Avoid starting too many large new chapters.

Mock analysis method

  1. Re-solve every incorrect and guessed question without looking at the solution.
  2. Label the reason: concept gap, formula recall, calculation, misread, time management or poor question selection.
  3. Revise the underlying concept and solve 5–10 similar questions.
  4. Write one prevention rule in your mistake notebook.
